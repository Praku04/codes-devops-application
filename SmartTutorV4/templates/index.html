About SmartTutor AI

🎯 Platform Overview

SmartTutor AI is an intelligent, cross-domain learning management platform designed to revolutionize training and education across multiple industries including Operations, Healthcare, Finance, Technology, Manufacturing, and more.

👥 User Roles & Responsibilities

⚙️ Super Admin

  • Manage all users, trainers, and trainees across the platform
  • Oversee platform-wide analytics and comprehensive reporting
  • Approve and manage trainer registrations
  • Configure processes/teams across the organization
  • Access system logs, activity monitoring, and audit trails
  • Configure platform settings, permissions, and security
  • Monitor system performance and resource utilization

👨‍🏫 Trainer

  • Create and curate learning content from various sources
  • Design custom assessments with MCQ and subjective questions
  • Publish courses and manage trainee enrollments
  • Monitor trainee progress and performance metrics
  • Approve or reject assessment attempt requests
  • Review and provide detailed feedback on trainee submissions
  • Generate reports on trainee performance and course effectiveness

🎓 Trainee

  • Access personalized learning modules and courses
  • Take AI-powered assessments with instant feedback
  • Track learning progress and performance analytics
  • Interact with AI tutor for clarifications and guidance
  • Request additional assessment attempts when needed
  • View detailed explanations for assessment answers
  • Access course materials including documents and presentations

✨ Key Features

  • AI-Powered Learning: Intelligent content generation and personalized feedback
  • Multi-Format Support: PDF, DOCX, PPTX, video, and more
  • Smart Assessments: Automated evaluation with detailed explanations
  • Knowledge Base: Searchable repository with semantic search
  • Real-Time Analytics: Comprehensive dashboards and insights
  • Cross-Domain: Adaptable to any industry or subject matter
