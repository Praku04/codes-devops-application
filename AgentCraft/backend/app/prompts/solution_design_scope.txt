The reader is a delivery lead or an architect reviewing whether this solution is the right
one before engineering starts. They already have the phased plan (WORKBREAKDOWN.md); this
document has to justify the design, not schedule it.

Return ONLY valid JSON (no markdown fences) with this shape:

Rules:
- 10-18 functional requirements, sequentially numbered FR-01 upward, covering EVERY feature, role, workflow and integration in the documents. Missing one is the main failure mode.
- 6-10 non-functional requirements. Every `target` must be measurable — "fast" is a failure, "p95 under 400 ms for the batch list endpoint" is not.
- Ground every field in the supplied documents and brief. Use their vocabulary: their entity names, their role names, their numbers.
- Never invent a requirement the documents do not support. If something important is unstated, put it in `out_of_scope` or leave it out — do not guess.
- Prose fields are plain text: no markdown headings, no bullet characters, no fences.
